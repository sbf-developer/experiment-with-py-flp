NIGHT GRAVE V2 — atmospheric dark phonk
144 BPM, D Phrygian, 88 bars, approximately 2:27 plus reverb tails.
Open NIGHT GRAVE V2.flp. The complete project ZIP includes all samples.

Changes from V1:
- Locked four-on-the-floor kick, coordinated bass pulses with a 29 ms
  gap after each kick, stable clap backbeat, reduced fills and hat rolls.
- New simple repeating cowbell motif, with restrained melodic answers.
- Confirmed oscillator pitches for cowbell, sub, reese and pad instead
  of tuning ambiguous samples from the loudest FFT peak.
- Native Fruity Reeverb 2 filtered space on clap (12% slot blend),
  cowbell (23%) and pad (42%). All three effects are editable.
- Filtered phrase-end delays at 312.5/625/937.5 ms, quieter transitions,
  detuned atmospheric pad, mono controlled bass, and less crowded drops.
- Mixer inserts are named to match the instruments.

The drum sounds come from the installed Phonk and ModeAudio packs.
The newly designed pitched instruments are supplied as sampler assets.
Every musical part remains editable in the piano roll; envelope, filter,
pitch, mixer and reverb controls are available in the FLP.

The WAV and MP3 are actual renders from FL Studio 24.2.2.4597.
See production.json for source assets and validation.json for render checks.

Reference direction: dark drift-phonk and atmospheric phonk, including
Kordhell — Murder In My Mind and INTERWORLD — METAMORPHOSIS.
Reference links:
https://kordhell.bandcamp.com/album/murder-in-my-mind
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lJvRohYSrZM
The melody and arrangement are original.
